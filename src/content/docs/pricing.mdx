## Frequently asked

### Does my license expire?

No. Once you buy Pro, it's yours — permanently, and so are all the 1.x updates that follow.
There's nothing to renew for your major version. If Atrium ever ships a 2.0, that would be a new
purchase — but your v1 license keeps working exactly as it does today, forever.

### Is Free actually free, or a trial?

Free is free, forever, with no caps. It's the same organizing core described above — no feature
countdowns, no locked panes, no "upgrade to unlock" popups. Pro is additive, not a key that
unlocks a crippled app.

### Is there a subscription?

No. Atrium has no subscription billing, and never will. You pay once for Pro and get every
1.x update forever — no renewal, no update window, no subscription infrastructure of any kind.

### Why does the Pro app embed an Atlassian OAuth client secret?

Because Atlassian's OAuth flow requires one, and Atrium has no server to hide it on — there is
no Atrium backend, by promise. The embedded "secret" is not load-bearing: the actual security
boundary is PKCE plus a localhost-only callback, so extracting the string from the binary gets
you nothing — it can't read anyone's Jira data without the user completing Atlassian's own
consent screen. This is a deliberate, documented trade-off in favor of "no backend, ever," not
an oversight. (Every API call goes directly from your machine to Atlassian's; nothing routes
through us — see the [privacy policy](/atrium-site/privacy/).)

### How does checkout work?

Atrium sells through [Polar](https://polar.sh), a merchant of record — they handle payment,
tax/VAT, and your license key delivery. The buy button above goes straight to their hosted
checkout; no account with us is created (there is no "us" server to create one on). Your key
arrives by email and stays retrievable in Polar's customer portal.

## Frequently asked

### Does my license expire?

No. Once you buy Pro, it's yours — permanently, and so are all the 1.x updates that follow.
There's nothing to renew for your major version. If Atrium ever ships a 2.0, that would be a new
purchase — but your v1 license keeps working exactly as it does today, forever.

### Is Free actually free, or a trial?

Free is free, forever, with no caps. It's the same organizing core described above — no feature
countdowns, no locked panes, no "upgrade to unlock" popups. Pro is additive, not a key that
unlocks a crippled app.

### Is there a subscription?

No. Atrium has no subscription billing, and never will. You pay once for Pro and get every
1.x update forever — no renewal, no update window, no subscription infrastructure of any kind.

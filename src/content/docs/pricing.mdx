## Frequently asked

### Does my license expire?

No. Once you buy Pro, it's yours — permanently. What can lapse is the **update entitlement**: if
you don't renew after your first year, your license keeps working forever, you just stop
receiving new Pro-feature updates until you renew.

### Is Free actually free, or a trial?

Free is free, forever, with no caps. It's the same organizing core described above — no feature
countdowns, no locked panes, no "upgrade to unlock" popups. Pro is additive, not a key that
unlocks a crippled app.

### Is there a subscription?

No. Atrium has no subscription billing, and never will. You pay once for Pro; the update window
is time-limited, the license is not.
